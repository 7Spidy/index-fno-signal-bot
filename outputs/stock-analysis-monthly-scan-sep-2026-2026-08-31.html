Physical settlement. Four of the five are stock options (HDFCBANK, AUROPHARMA, ICICIBANK, PAYTM) and are physically settled. Every stock idea below therefore carries an exit-by date of Thu 24 Sep 2026, well clear of settlement week. Only the NIFTY spread is cash-settled.

Overlap disclosure — information, not a constraint; none of these ideas was dropped for resembling another, and each stands on its own:

  • #1 and #3 share a driver. HDFC Bank is the single largest NIFTY constituent, so the HDFCBANK put spread and the NIFTY put spread will tend to win together and lose together. Taking both doubles the same bet.
  • #1 and #4 are opposite sides of one sector. Short HDFCBANK against long ICICIBANK is a divergence trade inside private banks, backed by divergent fundamentals (below). It is not a hedge: a sector-wide re-rating in either direction can drag both legs the wrong way.
  • Direction is skewed long. Three of five are bullish. #5 PAYTM carries the highest index sensitivity in the list (60-session beta 1.71) and works directly against #3.
  • #2 is the only index-independent idea here — AUROPHARMA's 60-session beta to NIFTY is −0.39, i.e. it has been moving counter to the index.
