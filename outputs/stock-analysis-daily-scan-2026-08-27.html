How the screen ran

All 213 September stock futures were pulled live in one snapshot, then filtered and classified — no third-party screener, no pre-baked list.

  • Liquidity floor: ≥ ₹150 cr of futures notional traded today. That cut 213 → 90 names. Indices excluded ("stocks", as asked).
  • Buildup classification: price change vs prior close, cross-referenced against where current OI sits inside its own intraday range. OI pinned at the day's high with price down = short buildup; at the high with price up = long buildup.
  • Then, per finalist: daily candles for 20/50-DMA, RSI(14), MACD, ATR(14) and realised vol; the full SEP option chain for OI shifts and bid-ask; and Black-76 implied vol and delta solved from live premiums.

🚩 200-DMA is not quoted anywhere in this document. Fetching 200 sessions for every finalist was not feasible inside this run's budget, so the trend reads use 20/50-DMA plus 60-day structure. Where a name's history is short the figure would have been unreliable anyway. Treat every trend call here as short-to-medium term.

🚩 IV percentile against each stock's own recent range is also not quoted — that needs a historical implied-vol series the Kite endpoints don't expose. The implied-versus-realised spread is used instead. It is computed from live premiums and actual candles, and it is the number that decides whether buying premium is the right structure.

Sizing, correlation and events

The per-idea 10% cap does not survive taking all six. Each size above caps that single idea at ~10% of the stated capital. One lot of each is ₹90,116 of premium — that is 90% of a ₹1 lakh account. To run the whole book with each leg inside its own cap you need roughly ₹3,50,000 of risk capital, at which point all six together commit about 26%. These are six independent candidates to choose among, not a portfolio to buy wholesale.

Correlation to watch: KOTAKBANK and LICHSGFIN are both lenders and will trade together on a rate or credit headline. BHEL and the dropped ADANIPOWER are the same capex theme. MUTHOOTFIN carries the MANAPPURAM correlation described above. Three long and three short is a deliberate balance against a flat index, not a hedge — the shorts will not protect the longs if the whole tape turns.

Events: Q1 FY27 results are done across this set — the reporting season ran through early August — and no scheduled earnings fall inside the 3–5 session window for any of the six. That said, no per-name corporate-action calendar was independently verified in this run, and unscheduled board meetings and dividend announcements are common. Check NSE corporate announcements for your chosen name on the morning of entry.

Settlement: all six are NSE stock options and therefore physically settled. At a 3–5 session horizon this is irrelevant, but any position carried toward 29 Sep must be squared off — taking delivery on a 2,625-share BHEL lot or a 275-share MUTHOOTFIN lot is not a theoretical risk.
