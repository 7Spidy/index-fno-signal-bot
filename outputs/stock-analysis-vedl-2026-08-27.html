The brief

What trades as "VEDL" today is not the old Vedanta. The demerger went ex on 30 Apr 2026 and completed 15 Jun 2026, spinning out Aluminium, Oil & Gas, Power and Iron & Steel. The residual company is a zinc-silver-copper business anchored on a 60.7% stake in Hindustan Zinc, plus Zinc International (Gamsberg, Black Mountain, Skorpion), copper smelting, nickel and ferroalloys.

Q1 FY27 — the first clean post-demerger quarter — showed revenue of ₹23,456 cr (−1% QoQ) and PAT of ₹5,473 cr, down 18% sequentially on higher input costs. Note the shape of that number: Hindustan Zinc alone earned ₹5,469 cr (revenue ₹13,033 cr, +71.6% YoY). Strip out minorities and VEDL is substantially a leveraged holding vehicle on HZL, with the copper and international-zinc assets as the swing factor.

Three strengths: HZL is a first-quartile-cost, 25-year-mine-life zinc-silver asset; the parent's 54.72% share encumbrance was fully released on 21 Aug 2026 after Vedanta Resources repaid its facilities, removing a long-standing overhang; returns remain high (ROE 32.4% TTM, ROCE 58.0% — both flagged below).

Two watch-points: leverage is still real (D/E 1.82 TTM, improved from 2.37) and the holdco structure means group cash calls land here; and the restructuring is not finished — the board approved a further property-business demerger at the Q1 results, so corporate-action headlines keep arriving.

The derivatives confirm it rather than contradict it. Why a long put, and not the alternatives

Implied vol is trading below realised on every window — ATM IV is 27.5% against realised of 37.6% (10d), 32.6% (20d) and 30.4% (30d) — so movement is cheap here. That kills the case for anything short-premium, and it makes a bear put spread self-defeating: selling the 265 leg hands away the cheapest vega on the board to save ₹3.40, and caps the payoff at 2.05:1 held to expiry versus 2.56:1 on the naked put in four sessions. Short SEP futures expresses the same view with undefined gap risk and only 1.82:1 to the same target. The 275 strike over the 270 (R:R 2.69:1) is a deliberate liquidity trade-off: 275PE turned over 3,477 lots today against 1,372, on a 1.6% spread against 2.1%, and its higher delta captures the first leg into the moving-average cluster better.

Read the stop honestly. The ₹4.48 backstop sits only about 4–5 futures points above entry — roughly half of one ATR (₹8.05). It will do its job on a real reversal, and it will also stop you out on ordinary noise. That is the cost of buying a 33-day put with a 4-session horizon, and it is why R:R looks generous on the backstop (4.40:1) and merely adequate on the technical level (2.56:1). Treat 2.56:1 as the number that matters.
The 1:2 gate is met only on the full target. The first magnet down is the 20/50-DMA cluster at ₹273.3, which is a likely bounce zone — at that interim level R:R is about 0.97:1 on the technical stop. Book partial there (275PE ≈ ₹9.71, +54%) but understand that the published 2.56:1 requires the cluster to break and the move to extend toward ₹265. ₹265 is a 1.2-sigma move over 4 sessions on current realised vol. Achievable, not a base case.
